## Cross-computer workflow and remaining verification

The supported workflow is sequential: acquire the exclusive shared marker,
compare paired hashes with the recorded common ancestor, refresh a safe local
replica, save under the writer lease, then drain accepted saves at orderly close,
publish a complete immutable release/manifest and release the marker. Unknown
or divergent copies are conflicts. Another writer makes the second computer
read-only; marker age never authorizes forced reclamation. Local publication
does not prove OneDrive server upload or arrival on the other computer.

The desktop must independently preserve its runtime/profile/data, build the
exact final repair pin through governed packaging, inspect its existing selectors
and governed release identity read-only, and first inspect a separate hash-matched
replica with read-only verification. No desktop testing was performed here.
