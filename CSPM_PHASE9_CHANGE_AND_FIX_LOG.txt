FIX 001: Offline Sync Dialog Freeze Risk
- Problem: The native QMessageBox used in `sync_service.py` to prompt the user during an offline conflict could appear behind the splash screen or be buried under other windows, causing an apparent startup deadlock.
- Root Cause: `ctypes.windll.user32.MessageBoxW` was called with `hWnd=0` and default flags, which does not guarantee topmost visibility.

- Compatibility Assessment: Fully compatible. Native win32 MessageBox behaviour remains the same but with elevated Z-order.
